# The tracer computes light from column spans and angular shadow sweeps

The old tracer marched each sun/lamp ray through the grid one cell at a time (DDA over the 220×240 cells), reading every cell it crossed. The rewritten tracer precomputes the scene once per frame: per-column contiguous spans of occluder, water, and glass cells (`SceneSpans`), and per light an angular shadow sweep (`ShadowSweep`) — each light-blocking cell becomes a disc occluder of the cell's circumradius as seen from the light, and a sweep over the discs' angular intervals (a min-heap lower envelope) records which disc a ray at each angle hits first. A cell's light sample is then direct next-event estimation to the sun and the lit lamps: visibility is one angle lookup (binary search over the sweep's envelope), Beer–Lambert attenuation is the geometric length inside the water/glass spans, plus the surface glint lobe, the sun caustic gathered from refracted surface rays, and single-scatter shafts. Rays no longer march cells.

**Why:** the march's cost grows with the ray's length in cells, so a ray from the off-grid sun across the whole world reads up to ~460 cells, and the 2,500-ray frame budget bought correspondingly coarse coverage. The span/sweep form prices a ray at the columns it crosses (about its width) plus one O(log B) angle lookup, which is 10–100× cheaper on long rays and makes the per-frame cost predictable: the budget of 2,500 samples now covers the field many times over at the same 33 ms frame budget. The precompute (a full span rebuild is a few milliseconds; a per-light sweep is O(B log B) with B the occluder count) is paid once per frame and is dominated by nothing else in the tick.

**Consequences:** the traced view is unchanged in behaviour — all eight Phase 5 contract tests pass unmodified (one-cell partitions seal, furniture passes light, the roofed pool's surface is static across the sun's arc, the open pool's glint peaks under the sun, a submerged lamp reads warm amber, the field settles and re-converges only in the dirty box, a falling lamp stays inside the ray budget, and the budget falls back on sustained overrun and recovers) — and the per-sample cost drops, so the 2,500-ray cap keeps its meaning (a sustained 2 s window over the cap falls back, a window back under recovers) with more headroom than the march had. The sweep's disc occluders over-shadow a ray by at most the diagonal corner sliver of a cell (the disc contains the cell square, so no ray that crosses a blocking cell is ever visible — a safe direction); the corner case where a ray clips the disc's sliver without crossing the cell is the only over-shadow, and it is a sub-cell sliver at the cell corner. The sweep rebuilds whenever the sun moves (every frame in the traced view) and the lamp sweeps rebuild only when a lamp's lit state changes; the span rebuilds are diffed per column. Determinism is preserved: the sweep's min-heap breaks ties on the packed cell index, so a rebuild yields the identical envelope, and the sample remains a pure function of (world, water, lamps, t).
